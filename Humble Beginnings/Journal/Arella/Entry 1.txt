== Arella's Journal ==

I do not know how much longer I can burn it before there is nothing left of me to take back.

The incense is old—older than my bloodline, perhaps older than the gods who once watched over us. When I inhale it, something ancient stirs within me. It dulls pain, sharpens instinct, and turns fear into something almost foreign. Wounds become distant. Thought becomes unnecessary. Friend and foe become indistinguishable.

When I exhale, the smoke spills from my mask and blankets the room in its unnatural haze. But it does not possess those who breathe it secondhand. To them, it is only smoke—thick, bitter, and difficult to see through. I am the vessel. I am the one it chooses.

Every time I surrender to it, I feel that ancient presence settle deeper inside me.

I fear the day it no longer leaves.

But until then, I will burn it.

I will become the monster if that is what it takes.

My kin need me.

And I will protect them…
Whatever the cost.
