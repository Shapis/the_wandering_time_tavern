I have been poisoned.

I write those words only because I do not know what else to call the sickness spreading beneath my skin. The wound glows violet where the poison entered, and with every passing hour, I can feel something wrong moving through me. I have never experienced this before. I do not know how long I have before it reaches something vital, nor whether it can be removed at all.

The crimson draught was given to me for emergencies, though I never imagined I would need it for this. It is not an antidote. It is a sleeping draught, strengthened with alchemical blood-restoratives. When taken, it slows the body nearly to death—heartbeat, breath, blood flow. Even the poison is forced into stillness. It is dangerous, but it may buy me time. I may appear dead while I sleep.

I do not know how long that sleep will last.

I do not know if I will wake.

And I do not know whether the draught will be enough to keep the poison from taking me.

Perhaps that is what frightens me most.

Not death.

The thought that I may die before I can return to my kin.

I cannot let that happen.

Whatever this poison does to me, whatever price I must pay, I will endure it.

I have survived worse things than fear.

And I will survive this.

For them.
