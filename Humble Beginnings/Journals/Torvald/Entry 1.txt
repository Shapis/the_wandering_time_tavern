I don't usually write things down. Never saw much point in it. If something matters, I remember it. If it doesn't, I forget it. Tonight, however, seems worth remembering.

There was a fight in the tavern. The elf was surrounded. I helped. The little man helped too. He's strange. Not the sort of strange I usually mind.

He talks too much, but he knows when to stop. There is something behind his eyes that I don't understand, though I suspect he doesn't understand it either.

He was carrying a letter. Wouldn't say who it was from. Wouldn't say what was in it. Said he was hired to deliver it to the elf.

Fair enough. Not my business.

Until I decided to follow him.

I'm not entirely sure why. Perhaps I was bored. Perhaps I was curious. Perhaps I simply disliked the thought of letting someone that small walk into whatever trouble was waiting for him alone.

The elf had already disappeared into the night by then.

The little man intends to find her.

So do I.

I suppose that makes three of us now.

Whatever is waiting down that road, I have a feeling it won't be boring.

And that is probably reason enough to follow.
