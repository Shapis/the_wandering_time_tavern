== Vesper's Journal ==

As I entered the tavern. My only thought was avoiding the cold from the storm. I’m not usually one to walk into trouble. I know the cost that I must bear for doing so. And I’ve walked away from missions before if it suited me. But I was drawn to that elf for more reasons than contractual obligations, I don’t think she realizes that yet. And  I’m also not sure what to make of that yet.
